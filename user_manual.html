Help

Troubleshooting

Quick solutions to common operational issues.

Calls Ending Unexpectedly

Check your carrier SIP registration and ensure your balance is sufficient. Check the "Logs" tab for specific disconnect reason codes.

No Audio Issues

Ensure browser microphone permissions are enabled. Refresh the page to re-initialize the WebRTC audio context.

Dashboard Loading Problems

Clear browser cache or try in Incognito mode. Ensure your firewall is not blocking the WebSocket domain connection.

Connection Failures

Verify internet connection. If using a VPN, try disconnecting or whitelisting the platform domain.

Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from enterprise administrators.

  • Can I create multiple AI agents?

    Yes, enterprise accounts can create unlimited agents with different voices and logic profiles.

  • How do I export reports?

    Go to the "Reports & Analytics" section and click the "Export" button in the top right corner.

  • Does it support inbound and outbound calls?

    Yes, the platform handles both automated outbound campaigns and inbound customer service routing.

  • Is custom voice creation supported?

    Yes, you can upload a 30-second audio clip of your voice to create a clone (requires Enterprise Add-on).
